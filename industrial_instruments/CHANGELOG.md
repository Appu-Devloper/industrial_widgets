## 0.2.0

Breaking update focused on measurement and equipment instruments.

* Expand to 33 instruments and editable parameter presets without inferred operating limits.
* Add configurable radial sweep, explicit pressure conventions and secondary scale labels.
* Add three-phase values, probe, horizontal vessel, sight glass, flow/speed/position/angle instruments, center-zero/deviation scales and contact signal.
* Rename ArcGauge → ArcMeter, LinearMeter → LinearScale, SiloLevel → SiloIndicator and FlowPipe → PipeFlow.
* Replace battery charging flag with BatteryActivity and optional numeric display.
* Remove chart, sparkline, bullet graph and chart-data APIs. See doc/MIGRATION.md.
* Replace the gallery with a measurement-category catalog and editable ranges/units.
